Cork and Pin

Privacy

Plain-language version: we collect as little as possible, use it only to run the page you're using, and never sell it.

What's collected

  • When you buy squares or sign up for something: your name and a phone number or email, so the organizers can reach you (for example, if you win).
  • For online orders: which squares you picked, the order amount, and when you said you paid.
  • Basic technical information needed to run the site and prevent abuse, such as a scrambled (hashed) version of your network address.

Who sees it

  • On public boards, only your first name and last initial are shown.
  • Your full name and contact details are visible only to the organizers running that page (for example, your school's PTA).
  • It's never sold or shared for advertising.

Payments

Cork and Pin doesn't handle money. Payments go directly from you to the group running the page (for example, through Venmo), under that service's own terms.

Cookies and tracking

No advertising or tracking cookies. Pages may save small settings in your browser, such as staying signed in on a helper's tablet.

Removing your information

Each event's information is deleted when the organizers reset it for the next season. To have your details removed sooner, email info@corkandpin.com.
